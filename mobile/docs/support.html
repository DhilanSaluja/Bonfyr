Common questions

How do I contact Bonfyr?

Email dhilan.saluja@gmail.com with your Apple ID email (or the address you signed in with) and a short description of the issue. We read every message. Typical reply time is 1–2 business days.

How do I sign in?

Open Bonfyr and tap Sign in with Apple (recommended) or Sign in with Google. Hide My Email is fine. There is no username or password to remember.

How do I cancel or manage Bonfyr Pro?

Bonfyr Pro is billed by Apple. On iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Bonfyr, or open Bonfyr → You → Go Pro → manage in the App Store. Restore purchases is on that same screen if you reinstall.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: You → Delete account. That removes your account and associated personal data we are not legally required to keep, including Crews you own. You can also email us and we’ll help.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile or content and use Report or Block. For urgent safety issues, email us at dhilan.saluja@gmail.com with as much detail as you can.

Is Bonfyr private?

There is no public discovery feed. You only see people in your crews. Read the full Privacy Policy for what we collect and how to control it.
