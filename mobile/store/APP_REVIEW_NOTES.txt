2) DESCRIPTION (paste into Description)
============================================================
Bonfyr is THE app that keeps you and your friends together.

Every 24 hours, someone in your crew has to post a picture or video, chat, or start a Spark (a hangout that actually happens in real life). If nobody kindles, the fire goes out. That’s the point — it stays about right now, not a feed you never catch up on.

Start a Spark when you want to do something. Your crews get pinged so people can jump in. Keep the fire lit with photos, short videos, and chat that burn out after a day.

Free
• Create crews and invite friends with a code
• Start Sparks that go live now
• Photos, videos, GIFs, polls, and crew chat
• Find friends already on Bonfyr from your contacts

Bonfyr Pro (auto-renewable, $1.99/mo or $19.99/yr)
• Unlimited crews (Free is capped at 5)
• Schedule Sparks up to 24 hours ahead
• Pro badge on your profile

Sign in with Apple or Google. No public discovery feed — you only see people in your crews.

Privacy Policy and Terms are in the app under You → Privacy / Terms. Delete your account anytime under You → Delete account.

7) APP REVIEW INFORMATION — Notes (paste into Review Notes)
============================================================
Sign in: no username/password. Use Sign in with Apple (preferred).

Sign in with Apple uses the name and email from Authentication Services. The app does not ask you to type those again.

1. Launch Bonfyr → Sign in
2. Tap Sign in with Apple
3. Hide My Email is fine
4. On the optional setup screen, tap Continue. Photo and phone are optional.
5. Contacts, location, and notifications are requested later only if you use those features. Deny is fine — the app still works.

Any new Apple ID can create a fresh profile. No demo password.
