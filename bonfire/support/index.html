Common questions

How do I contact Bonfyr?

Email dhilan.saluja@gmail.com with the address you signed in with and a short description of the issue. We read every message.

How do I sign in?

Open Bonfyr and tap Sign in with Apple (recommended) or Sign in with Google. Hide My Email is fine. There is no username or password to remember.

How do I cancel or manage Bonfyr Pro?

Bonfyr Pro is billed by Apple. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bonfyr. Or in the app: You → Go Pro → manage in the App Store. Restore purchases is on that same screen if you reinstall.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: You → Delete account. That removes your account and associated personal data we are not legally required to keep, including Crews you own. Email us if you want help.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile or content and use Report or Block. For urgent safety issues, email us with as much detail as you can.

Is Bonfyr private?

There is no public discovery feed. You only see people in your crews. The Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how to control it.
