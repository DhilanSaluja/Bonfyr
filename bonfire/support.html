How do I sign in?

Open Bonfyr and use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Hide My Email is fine. There is no username or password to remember.

How do I manage Bonfyr Pro?

Bonfyr Pro is billed by Apple. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bonfyr. In the app: You → Go Pro. Restore purchases is on that same screen if you reinstall.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: You → Delete account. That removes your account and associated personal data we are not legally required to keep, including Crews you own. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on the account and we will help.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile or content and use Report or Block. For something urgent, email us with as much detail as you can.

Where is the Privacy Policy?

The Privacy Policy explains what we collect, who can see your posts, and how to ask us to delete your information. This website does not set cookies or run analytics.
