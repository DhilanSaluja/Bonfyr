Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Bonfyr (“we”) respects your privacy. This page explains what the Bonfyr mobile app collects, how it is used, and how you can ask us about your data.

On this page
  1. What we collect
  2. How we use it
  3. Sharing
  4. Retention and deletion
  5. Your choices
  6. Children
  7. Contact

1. What we collect

We collect only what we need to run Bonfyr:

  • Account info: name, email from Apple or Google, and optional phone and photo.
  • Crew and Spark content you post: photos, videos, chat, and captions.
  • Device push tokens, so we can send notifications you allow.
  • Approximate location only when you choose to share a meetup spot with a Spark.
  • Contact phone hashes when you opt in to Find Friends. Your address book stays on your device. We only receive hashed phone numbers for matching.

2. How we use it

We use this information to run Bonfyr: show your profile, Crews, Sparks, photos, and chat; match friends; send notifications you allow; process subscriptions; moderate reports; and keep the product secure and working.

3. Sharing

Content you post is visible to members of the Crews you share with. We use service providers such as Supabase (backend, auth, storage), Apple and Google (sign-in and in-app purchases), Expo (push), and Google Maps (meetup maps when you share a spot). We do not sell your personal information.

4. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app under You → Delete account. That removes your account and associated personal data we are not legally required to keep, including Crews you own. Chat and photos expire on a short schedule (about 24 hours) even if you stay on the app.

5. Your choices

You can skip contacts, deny location, and turn off notifications in system settings. You can report or block other users from their content. Contact us to ask questions about your data.

6. Children

Bonfyr is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
